DETAILS

Frequently asked questions

Service standards, pricing, airport procedures, and business accounts.

How do airport transfers work?

We monitor your flight in real time and adjust pickup automatically. Domestic and international airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting time, with meet-and-greet available on request.

Can I schedule a ride weeks in advance?

Yes. RYDEPRO supports scheduled bookings weeks ahead as well as same-day requests. Recurring and hourly reservations are available for both personal and business travel.

Do you offer corporate billing and expense integration?

Yes. Business accounts receive centralized billing, trip history, and expense integration. Administrators can manage riders, set policies, and export reporting from a single dashboard.

What vehicles are available?

Premium includes Sedan, E-Sedan, and Minivan. Executive and Luxury each include Sedan, E-Sedan, and SUV. Commercial Buses has one Bus option. Group and multi-vehicle bookings are supported under one reservation.

How do driver background checks work?

Every driver completes a three-level verification: an FBI identity history summary (rap sheet), a state DOJ background check, and a third-party screening. Driver records are re-verified on an ongoing basis.

How do Fixed and Auction pricing differ?

Fixed pricing locks in a predetermined rate before booking. Auction pricing lets you set a maximum, and the marketplace works toward a match below that ceiling. Both options are transparent — you always see your price before confirming.

How do member credits and tiers work?

Members earn credits on every eligible ride. Credits scale across 10 tiers. Higher tiers unlock priority dispatch, preferred vehicles, and dedicated support. Tiers 1–3 earn base credits, tiers 4–7 unlock higher multipliers, and tiers 8–10 receive maximum multipliers and priority access to new markets. Referral credits are earned on every new member.

When is on-demand ride auction launching?

On-demand ride auction is currently in development and will roll out worldwide following our U.S., Europe, and Canada launch. Join the waitlist to be notified when it becomes available in your market.
